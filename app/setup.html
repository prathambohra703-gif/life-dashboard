Life Dashboard

Setup

Follow this top to bottom, one step at a time. About 45 minutes, once. Every Copy button puts text on your clipboard to paste somewhere else. Ticked parts are remembered on this device.

Part 0 of 7 · 2 minutes

What you need

  • A Google account.
  • An iPhone with Safari, on iOS 16.4 or later.
  • A laptop with Chrome.
  • 45 minutes, once.

Do Parts 1 to 3 first. Sync, offline use and export all work after that. The rest can wait. Button names on other sites may differ a little from what is written here.

Part 1 of 7 · 5 minutes

Open the app and import your data

  1. You are already here, so the app is online. On your laptop, open the app.
  2. Tap the Menu button (top right).
  3. Tap Data and backup.
  4. Under Import a backup file, choose life-dashboard-import.json, the file Claude gave you.
  5. Check the table. In file and Now in app must match on every row.
  6. If a title says needs a look, stop and send the list to Claude.

Imports only add. Records you already have are never overwritten.

Part 2 of 7 · 10 minutes

Create the cloud database

  1. Go to supabase.com/dashboard and sign up free.
  2. Press New project. Name it life-dashboard.
  3. Set Region to Mumbai (South Asia).
  4. Make a database password and save it somewhere safe. The app never needs it.
  5. Press Create new project. Wait about two minutes.
  6. Open SQL Editor, then New query.
  7. Copy the database script, paste it, and press Run.
    Copied
  8. Open Authentication, then Users.
  9. Press Add user, then Create new user.
  10. Type your email and a new password. Tick Auto Confirm User. Press Create user.
  11. Open Settings, then API Keys.
  12. Copy the Project URL and keep it. It is near the top of the page or under Data API.
  13. Copy the publishable key. It starts with sb_publishable_.

Never copy a secret key into the app or into this page.

Part 3 of 7 · 5 minutes

Connect laptop, then iPhone

  1. In the app on your laptop, tap the status pill at the top. It says On this device only.
  2. Paste the Project URL.
  3. Paste the publishable key into the key box. It is labelled Publishable key and starts with sb_publishable_.
  4. Type the email and password from Part 2.
  5. Press Connect and sync. The pill turns to Synced.
  6. On your iPhone, open this same site in Safari.
  7. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  8. Open the app from the new icon, not from Safari.
  9. Tap the status pill and enter the same four values. Press Connect and sync. Your data appears.
Part 4 of 7 · 15 minutes

Push reminders

  1. Press Generate keys. They are made on this device, shown once, and never stored. Keep this tab open until Part 6 is done.

    If you reload this page the keys are gone. Make new ones and start this part again.

  2. In Supabase, open Edge Functions, then Secrets.
  3. Add four secrets with exactly these names: VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY, VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY, APP_SECRET, CONTACT_EMAIL.
  4. Use the three values above. For CONTACT_EMAIL, use your email. Press Save.
  5. Open Edge Functions, then Deploy a new function, then Via Editor.
  6. Name it remind. Delete the sample code.
  7. Copy the reminder function, paste it, and press Deploy.
    Copied
  8. Open the remind function, then Details (or Settings).
  9. Turn OFF Verify JWT with legacy secret. Save.

    The function checks its own secret, and the push key and calendar feed are meant to be public.

  10. Open Database, then Extensions. Enable pg_cron and pg_net.
  11. Fill in these two boxes. The values stay in this page.
    Copied

  12. In SQL Editor, open New query, paste the schedule, and press Run. It checks for due reminders every 5 minutes.
  13. In the app, open Menu, then Reminders, then Turn on for this device. Tap Allow.
  14. Press Send a test. Do this on the laptop, then on the iPhone.

On iPhone this only works from the home-screen app, on iOS 16.4 or later.

Part 5 of 7 · 5 minutes

Calendar feed

  1. In the app, open Menu, then Calendar feed.
  2. Press Copy link. Keep the link private.
  3. iPhone: open Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar.
  4. Paste the link in Server, tap Next, then Save.
  5. Google Calendar on a computer: next to Other calendars click +, then From URL.
  6. Paste the link and click Add calendar.

Calendar apps check for changes every few hours. If the app is not connected yet, the sheet asks you to connect first.

Part 6 of 7 · 10 minutes

Daily Google Drive backup and nightly review inbox

  1. Go to script.google.com.
  2. Press New project. Delete the sample code.
  3. Copy the backup script and paste it.
    Copied
  4. Open Project Settings (the gear on the left).
  5. Under Script Properties, press Add script property and add four. Press Save script properties.
  6. FUNCTION_URL: your Project URL followed by /functions/v1/remind.
  7. PUBLISHABLE_KEY: the publishable key.
  8. APP_SECRET: the value from Part 4.
  9. EMAIL: the email from Part 2.
  10. Go back to Editor. In the toolbar, pick the function install.
  11. Press Run. Press Review permissions, choose your account, then Allow.
  12. Open Google Drive. Check for two folders: Life Dashboard Backups and Life Dashboard Inbox.
  13. Tell Claude: “Tell Claude the inbox folder exists.” Claude can then create the 12:30 AM review task.

A new backup file arrives every night at about 4:30 AM. The last 30 are kept.

Part 7 of 7 · 1 minute

Keep it alive

Free Supabase projects pause after a week with no activity. The app syncs daily and the Apps Script pings every day, so yours stays awake.

  1. If the status pill ever says Sync problem, open supabase.com.
  2. Open your project and press Restore project.
Optional · small cost

Claude for free-text logging

Skip this if you like. Short commands such as “water 500” work without it. With it, meal descriptions can be read too. It costs a little on your own Anthropic key.

  1. Create a key at console.anthropic.com.
  2. In Supabase, open Edge Functions → Secrets and add ANTHROPIC_API_KEY.
  3. Press Deploy a new function, then Via Editor. Name it ai.
  4. Paste this and press Deploy.
    Copied
  5. In the app, tap the status pill and tick Use Claude for free-text logging.
Troubleshooting

If something goes wrong

  • Pill says “Sync problem”. Open supabase.com, press Restore project, then tap the pill and Sync now.
  • No notification on iPhone. Open the app from the home-screen icon, not Safari. Use iOS 16.4 or later. Redo Turn on for this device.
  • Import says “needs a look”. Nothing was deleted. Send the list on screen to Claude.
  • Apps Script says “Set FUNCTION_URL…”. One of the four Script Properties is missing or misspelled. Add it and run install again.
  • Reminders or calendar give a 401 error. Verify JWT with legacy secret is still on. Turn it off for remind.
  • Calendar shows nothing for a day. The feed is cached for 15 minutes. Wait, then refresh the calendar.
  • Schedule script will not copy. Generate the keys first, then fill in both boxes in Part 4.
  • Reminders never arrive. Check that pg_cron and pg_net are enabled and the schedule ran without an error.
